Privacy Policy

Last updated: September 2, 2026

Information We Collect

Retake is designed with privacy in mind. We only collect the minimum information necessary for basic server operations.

What We Store

  • Favorite teams — so you can see them in the app.
  • Currently live games being watched — used to display live match data. This information is not linked to you in any way and is not user-identifiable.

What We Don't Collect

  • No names, emails, or account information (beyond the waitlist signup on this website, which is handled by a third party).
  • No device identifiers or analytics tracking.
  • No browsing history or usage data.
  • No location data.

Third-Party Services

Our website uses Formspree to handle waitlist email signups. Formspree's own privacy policy applies to that data.

Changes to This Policy

If we update this policy, we'll post the revised date above. Continued use of Retake after changes means you accept the updated policy.

Contact

If you have questions about this policy, feel free to reach out to us.
